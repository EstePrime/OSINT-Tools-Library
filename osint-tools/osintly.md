## What does the Tool Do?

Osintly is a unified OSINT platform that consolidates investigating across multiple identifiers and data types into a single workspace. Rather than opening separate tools for email searches, domain lookups, phone number investigations, and other OSINT tasks, analysts can conduct comprehensive searches from one interface.

**Key Capabilities:**

- **Unified Search**: Search across six target types simultaneously (pseudonyms, emails, phone numbers, IPs, domains, crypto addresses) with results streamed in real time
- **AI Analyst**: Chat-based analysis interface, timeline generation, signal filtering, and contextual web search
- **Real-Time Enrichment**: Automatic correlation and enrichment of search results across data sources
- **Monitoring**: Set automated monitors on queries with notifications via email, Discord, or Telegram
- **Projects & Organization**: Organize searches, files, and notes into cases for solo or team investigations
- **Team Collaboration**: Invite team members, assign roles, and share investigation credits
- **Export**: Generate investigation reports in PDF, JSON, or DOCX formats

## How to Use

1. **Create an account** at https://osint.ly (free tier available)
2. **Start a search** by entering an identifier (email, phone, username, IP, domain, or crypto address)
3. **Review results** from 900+ integrated OSINT modules in real time
4. **Use AI Analyst** to analyze findings, generate timelines, and filter by signal strength
5. **Set monitors** on ongoing investigations with alert notifications
6. **Export findings** in your preferred format (PDF, JSON, DOCX)
7. **Collaborate** with team members by sharing projects and credit across investigations

For detailed guides and walkthroughs, visit: http://docs.osint.ly/

## Ethical Considerations

Osintly maintains a formal Ethics Charter requiring users to adhere to strict standards:

**Prohibited Activities** (Terms of Service):
- **No Doxxing**: Publishing private information (addresses, phone numbers, family details) with malicious intent is strictly banned
- **No Stalking**: Systematic tracking or monitoring of individuals for harassment is prohibited
- **No Malicious Offensive Operations**: Using data for reconnaissance to plan cyberattacks, unauthorized intrusions, or social engineering is forbidden
- **No Harassment or Illegal Activity**: Violation results in immediate account termination without refund

**Core Ethical Principles** (Ethics Charter):
- **Proportionality & Minimization**: Collect only data necessary for legitimate investigation purposes
- **Verification & Cross-Referencing**: Corroborate findings across multiple independent sources before conclusions
- **Objectivity & Context**: Never manipulate, forge, or selectively omit data; avoid bias in analysis
- **Passive Collection Only**: OSINT is passive research only; active engagement (phishing, social engineering) using Osintly data is prohibited
- **Legal Boundaries**: Do not bypass authentication, crack passwords, or exploit vulnerabilities—this is hacking, not OSINT
- **Operational Security**: Protect your account and investigation integrity; be aware interaction with external infrastructure may expose your IP
- **Data Retention**: Delete investigation data once mission is complete; do not retain indefinitely
- **Publication Responsibility**: Ensure public sharing of findings serves public interest, not reputational damage

**Data Handling**:
- **GDPR Compliance**: Osintly commits to GDPR and data protection laws
- **Right to Removal**: Users can request removal of personal information from Osintly's index via contact@osint.ly (with identification)
- **Data Deletion**: You can delete searches/projects instantly, removing all associated data from databases and storage
- **Account Deletion**: Full account deletion is available via account settings
- **AI Data Processing**: AI features may process your research data; explicitly trigger AI actions to limit processing

**Legal & Responsible Use**:

- User is solely responsible for all searches and results usage
