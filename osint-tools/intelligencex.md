### What does IntelligenceX do?

IntelligenceX is a powerful OSINT search engine that indexes data leaks, breaches, and historical internet records. It lets you search beyond the visible web into paste sites, darknet content, and archived data dumps.

Key features:

* Searches emails, domains, IPs, URLs, and keywords.
* Indexes breach data and leaked datasets.
* Includes historical snapshots (like archived WHOIS, DNS, and files).
* Covers surface web, dark web, and paste sites.
* Lets you pivot between related data points.

**The lowdown:** The tool allows you to dig deep into leaks and historical data (great for uncovering hidden connections) but much of its valuable info is behind a paywall.

Cookies are mainly used for analytics and session management, tracking whether you’re a unique visitor (via Statcounter) and maintaining your session on IntelligenceX.

### Use in Reporting

IntelligenceX can be used to:

* Identify exposed emails, credentials, or leaked data.
* Link individuals or organisations to breaches.
* Discover historical presence of domains or content.
* Support investigations into cybercrime, fraud, or exposure.
* Pivot across datasets to uncover hidden relationships.

| **Capabilities**                                        | **Limitations**                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Deep search across breaches and leaks.                  | Most valuable features are behind a paywall.           |
| Historical internet data (archives, past records).      | Data can be sensitive, incomplete, or unverified.      |
| Multi-source indexing (clear web and dark web).         | Requires careful interpretation (risk of false links). |
| Strong pivoting between entities (email - domain - IP). | Interface can feel overwhelming for beginners.         |
| File and document discovery.                            | <p><br></p>                                            |
| Advanced filters for targeted investigations.           | <p><br></p>                                            |

### Summary

IntelligenceX is a deep-dive OSINT powerhouse for searching breaches, leaks, and historical data. It’s especially useful when standard search tools hit a wall, helping uncover hidden links and past activity and as such, is most useful in the collection and analysis stages of the OSINT workflow.

### Ownership

IntelligenceX (intelx.io) is owned and operated by the privately held European technology company Intelligence X, which was founded in 2018 by [Peter Kleissner](https://x.com/Kleissner) based in Prague, Czech Republic.

### Ethical Considerations

* Handle leaked and personal data responsibly.
* Do not misuse exposed credentials or sensitive info.
* Be aware of legal restrictions in your jurisdiction.
* Avoid harm when reporting on individuals.
* Verify findings as leaked data can be misleading or outdated.
